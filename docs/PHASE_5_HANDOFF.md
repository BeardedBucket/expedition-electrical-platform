## Documentation, boundaries and limitations

[PRODUCT_SELECTION.md](PRODUCT_SELECTION.md) explains ownership, evidence, exact
contracts, bounds, normalization versus calculation, missing versus explicit absence,
fixed/assembly behavior, replay, failure/snapshot ownership, Phase 4 and Phase 3
handoffs, extension points and future corpus metrics. Architecture/Phase 3/Phase 4
documentation links it. Near-code comments explain record review, contextual ratings,
binding consistency, inherited conditions, null witnesses, permission-constrained
reduction and ownership. No unexplained safety margin or standards value is introduced.

Operational policy limits are 512 models, 4,096 witnesses per concrete candidate,
100,000 total witnesses/explicit-intent admission, and 1,024 assembly units. They are
conservative configurable application bounds without empirical tuning, independent
of electrical safety and Phase 4 role bounds. Overflow rejects atomically, never truncates.

Remaining limitations are explicit: contextual constraint interpretation; heterogeneous
storage; unbounded bank search; final shared/concurrent ratings; installed isolation;
dispatch/source trajectories; usable/autonomy storage; conductor/protection/clearance/
ventilation/bonding synthesis; missing fixed records or ports for exact handoff; and
review/source-native facts unavailable in the supplied canonical corpus. Optional and
deferred scopes do not become requirements. Large corpus expansion and Phase 6 are
not implemented. Reason codes enable later audits by mismatch, missing fact, withheld
review, context, binding, explicit negative capability, upstream sizing, missing fixed
record and assembly permission/reduction, without inventing publication/ingestion causes.
